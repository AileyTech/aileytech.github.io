About Us

The Explanation Layer
Others Avoid Building

AILeyTech exists because AI is being used in decisions that affect real people — and those decisions need to be explainable, defensible, and human-accountable.

Our Mission

We help organizations prove, explain, and control AI-assisted decisions — before audits, after incidents, and at scale.

The world is adopting AI faster than it's building accountability infrastructure. We're here to close that gap — with production-grade tools, not policy documents.

Values

What We Believe

Transparency First

Every decision, every safeguard, every escalation — visible and auditable. We don't hide complexity, we explain it.

Defensibility Over Convenience

We'd rather slow a process down by one step than ship something an auditor can challenge. Defensible decisions are non-negotiable.

Human Accountability Always

AI assists. Humans decide. Every AI-touched decision in our platform has a human owner who is responsible.

Integrity in Every Interaction

From our voice agents to our evidence packs, we operate with the same rigor we ask our customers to adopt.
